It seems that frame navigation via the FrameNavigation Class and tk.raise() may be a good option.
The only thing is that I'm not sure if I like having a big, persistent navigation panel
on the side of the app screen. I had envisioned a GUI with separate, different screens
and perhaps a dropdown menu allowing the user to select their desired screen. Of course,
this may well still be possible, might just have to tinker with the NavigationClass/tk.raise() method

Seems that the place() method is better than grid() when you want to specify dimensions of a widget.
Allows for fractional resizing based on window size, i.e. the height of the frame is 10% the height
of the window.
